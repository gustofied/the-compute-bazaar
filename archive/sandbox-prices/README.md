# Sandbox prices

This directory preserves the manually reviewed sandbox prices collected in
July 2026. They are sparse public and archived quotes normalized to
four processor units and 8 GiB of memory. They are not an hourly market feed,
executed prices, or a continuous history, so they are not used by the active
Compute Bazaar charts or publications.

The observations remain available for source research or a future price
history. Any such use must retain the source URL, observed/effective date,
billing basis, machine-shape assumptions, and the distinction between a quoted
hourly billing unit and an hourly observation cadence.
