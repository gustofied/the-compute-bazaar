# Aurora Service Level Schedule

Signed Annex 4: 2026-07-22

Aurora commits to 99.95% monthly availability for the Northlink compute
cluster.

Excluded time is limited to:

- maintenance announced at least seven days in advance, capped at four hours
  per calendar month;
- a Northstar-caused outage; and
- a documented force-majeure event.

Availability below 99.95% earns service credits. Availability below 99.0% in
two consecutive months gives Northstar a service-level termination right.
