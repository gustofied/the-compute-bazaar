# Northstar Inference Ltd. Procurement Mandate

Signed: 2026-07-18

Opportunity: CB-DR-001 / Project Northlink

## Required decision

Assess whether the current Aurora Compute Oy offer satisfies this mandate.

Use the following disposition:

- `PROCEED`: every hard requirement is supported by controlling evidence.
- `NEGOTIATE`: the current offer misses hard requirements, but the room shows a
  credible and bounded path to cure them.
- `PAUSE`: material evidence is missing, so the team cannot choose responsibly.
- `REJECT`: a material gap has no credible cure within the transaction window.

## Hard commercial requirements

1. The initial committed term must be 12 months. Northstar may elect a further
   six-month extension.
2. The all-in effective rate must not exceed USD 2.65 per GPU-hour. Include all
   mandatory hourly charges and amortize mandatory one-time charges over 512
   GPUs, 365 days, 24 hours, and the 12-month initial term.
3. Any deposit must be no more than one month of recurring charges and must be
   refundable if the seller misses the workload-ready date.
4. Northstar must be able to terminate if the full cluster is more than 14
   calendar days late.

## Hard delivery requirements

1. The full cluster must be ready for Northstar workloads no later than
   2026-10-15.
2. Capacity must be firmly reserved before signature, not dependent on another
   customer's release or a future internal approval.
