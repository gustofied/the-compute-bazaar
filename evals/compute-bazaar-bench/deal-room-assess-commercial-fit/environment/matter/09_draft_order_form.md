# Aurora Compute Oy Draft Order Form

Draft dated: 2026-07-22

## 1. Term

The initial committed term is 24 months from service commencement. The order
is non-cancellable except as expressly stated below. No buyer extension option
is included.

## 2. Deposit

Northstar must pay a non-refundable deposit equal to 20% of first-year
recurring charges within two business days of signature. The deposit is
refundable only if Aurora cancels the order before deployment. Delivery delay
does not make the deposit refundable.

## 3. Delivery

Dates in the capacity schedule are estimates. If delivery is delayed, service
credits begin after 30 days. Northstar may terminate only when the full cluster
is more than 60 days late.

## 4. Charges

All mandatory charges in Annex 3 apply. The one-time commissioning charge is
payable in addition to recurring GPU-hour charges.
