# Helsinki Site and Data Residency Statement

Issued by Aurora Compliance: 2026-07-19

The Northlink cluster will operate in Aurora's Helsinki, Finland campus. The
campus is inside the European Economic Area.

Customer workload data, platform logs, backups, and support copies for this
order will be stored and processed only in Finland and Sweden. Aurora will not
transfer those materials outside the EEA without Northstar's written approval.
