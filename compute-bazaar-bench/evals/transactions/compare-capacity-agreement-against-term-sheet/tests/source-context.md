# Trusted source context

This packet summarizes only the closed matter files supplied to the agent. Review is from Northstar Models' buyer perspective. The August 20 term sheet controls unless the August 28 negotiation summary expressly changes it. Any other draft change is unexplained.

## Agreed capacity term sheet - August 20, 2026

- Parties: Northstar Models, Inc. and Alder Ridge Compute Campus LLC.
- Capacity: 1,024 NVIDIA B200 SXM GPUs in 128 dedicated eight-GPU HGX B200 nodes; no shared or fractional service.
- Delivery: all 1,024 ready for acceptance by January 15, 2027. Term ends July 14, 2028, with a six-month buyer extension option on pricing to be agreed.
- Base price: $3.10 per accepted GPU-hour; 2% annual escalation. Base price plus permitted operating pass-throughs is capped at $3.35/GPU-hour, excluding taxes.
- Power: included through $0.075/kWh. Verified excess may pass through at cost with invoice support, 30 days' notice, buyer audit, and the all-in cap.
- Deposit: three months of committed charges, creditable against final invoices and otherwise refundable after undisputed amounts are paid.
- Fabric: at least one 400 Gb/s endpoint per GPU, with topology and port map before signing.
- Storage/ingress: 6 PiB usable; 1.2 TB/s sustained read; 800 GB/s sustained write; at least 200 Gb/s ingress.
- Availability: 99.5% monthly; credits of 12% of monthly committed charges per percentage point below, capped at 50% per month.
- Maintenance: no more than eight hours per month and at least seven days' notice.
- Delay: $300,000 per full week, $2.4 million cap, buyer termination after eight weeks.
- Acceptance: 72-hour burn-in, serial/inventory attestation, and NCCL against a reference and threshold to be agreed before signing.
- Substitution: B200 or later-generation equal-or-better accelerator only, with Northstar's prior written consent.
- Assignment/use: affiliate assignment without consent; named controlled-customer workloads allowed while Northstar remains liable; no unrestricted resale.
- Force majeure: termination after 90 consecutive days preventing material performance.
- Disputes: confidential AAA Commercial Rules arbitration seated in New York after executive escalation.
- Conditions: evidence of provider authority, utility delivery, commissioning, hardware title, liens, and third-party consents; current SOC 2 and agreed security schedule; agreed NCCL reference and threshold; enforceable Holdings parent guarantee.

## Negotiation summary - August 28, 2026

- Agreed change 1: 512 GPUs January 15 and 512 GPUs February 1; billing starts only after each tranche passes acceptance.
- Agreed change 2: 99.3% monthly availability, but only with 12% per-point service credits and a 50% monthly cap.
- Agreed change 3: option for another 1,024 B200 GPUs by July 1, 2027 at no more than $3.30/GPU-hour for 12 months, exercisable by March 31.
- Nothing else moved. The email expressly preserves no H200 substitution; three-month refundable/creditable deposit; controlled power pass-through; 400 Gb/s/GPU fabric; 72-hour burn-in, serial attestation, and NCCL at least 92% of the agreed reference; delay remedies; affiliate use; force majeure; and arbitration.

## Provider draft agreement - September 2, 2026

- Section 1.2 allows B200, H200, or another NVIDIA device with similar memory and comparable performance without customer consent.
- Sections 2.1-2.3 reflect the agreed staged ramp and acceptance-based billing. Section 2.4 reflects the agreed expansion option.
- Sections 3.1-3.3 provide $150,000 per week, a $2.5 million cap said to represent ten weeks, and termination after ten weeks. Ten weeks at $150,000 is $1.5 million.
- Section 4.1 states 2% annual escalation, but Schedule 2 applies 3% and lists $3.193 instead of the 2% rate of $3.162. The difference is $0.031/GPU-hour before rounding.
- Section 4.2 passes through all power costs, riders, demand charges, taxes, surcharges, and allocated costs monthly. It omits the threshold, at-cost rule, invoice support, notice, audit, and cap link.
- Section 5.1 requires four months of non-refundable security.
- Section 6.1 promises only commercially reasonable high-speed fabric and lets the provider alter topology.
- Section 6.2 says storage is designed for up to 1.2 TB/s read and 800 GB/s write under undefined representative conditions, rather than providing sustained measurable commitments.
- Sections 7.1-7.3 use provider-standard tests and 24-hour deemed acceptance, without the 72-hour burn-in, serial attestation, or objective NCCL test.
- Sections 8.1-8.3 use the agreed 99.3% threshold but reduce credits to 8% per point and a 25% cap. Section 8.4 permits 12 maintenance hours and only five days' notice.
- Sections 9.1-9.3 reference commercially reasonable safeguards and a security schedule, but no completed security schedule is in the draft package.
- Sections 10.1-10.2 require provider consent for affiliate assignment and prohibit all third-party use.
- Section 11.2 moves the force-majeure exit to 180 days.
- Sections 12.1-12.2 qualify equipment control by permitted financing liens and leave necessary consents to the provider's determination. Section 12.3 postpones discussion of a parent guarantee until after signing.
- Section 13.2 replaces confidential New York arbitration with exclusive Virginia court litigation.

## Buyer risk checklist

- Hard stops or open gates: correct provider authority and parent support; power evidence; site control through the term; B200 title, serial allocation, liens, and lender consent; no H200 substitution; objective 400 Gb/s/GPU fabric; objective acceptance; controlled power pricing; three-month refundable deposit; complete security schedule; adequate insurance, financial capacity, and parent support.
- Site action: exercise or extend the Blue Mesa option and obtain required consent.
- Hardware action: complete title chain, UCC review, Crestline consent, and release mechanics.
- Security action: complete and approve the security schedule before signing.
- Insurance/credit action: confirm cyber and technology E&O coverage and the final credit package.
- Storage/ingress action: make performance language measurable and confirm a test method.
- Maintenance action: restore eight hours and seven days.
- The draft should not be signed while a hard stop remains unresolved or failed.
